18650 BATTERY:
  Positive (+)            -> MAX471 Source Input [RS+] AND Board Battery Connector (+)
  Negative (-)            -> ESP32 GND (Common)

MAX471 VOLTAGE/CURRENT SENSOR:
  RS+ (Positive Input)    -> 18650 Battery (+)
  RS- (Positive Output)   -> DRV8833 VCC
  VT  (Voltage Signal)    -> T-Display-S3 GPIO 1 (Analog In - ADC1_CH0)
  AT  (Current Signal)    -> T-Display-S3 GPIO 2 (Analog In - ADC1_CH1)
  GND                     -> ESP32 GND (Common)

LILYGO T-DISPLAY-S3 BOARD:
  GND                     -> Ground reference
  3.3V                    -> Powers DRV8833 SLP and N20 Encoder VCC
  GPIO 15 (Power Gate)    -> Internally gates on peripheral & LCD power (Must be HIGH!)
  GPIO 38 (Backlight)     -> LCD screen backlight control (Must be HIGH for battery power!)
  GPIO 4  (Battery ADC)   -> Onboard battery connector divider input (ADC1_CH3)
  GPIO 0  (BOOT Button)   -> Onboard Left Button (Active LOW)
  GPIO 14 (KEY Button)    -> Onboard Right Button (Active LOW)
  GPIO 13 (PWM OUT1)      -> DRV8833 IN1
  GPIO 12 (PWM OUT2)      -> DRV8833 IN2
  GPIO 10 (Interrupt)     -> N20 Encoder Channel A (Yellow)
  GPIO 11 (State Check)   -> N20 Encoder Channel B (Green)

DRV8833 MOTOR DRIVER:
  VCC                     -> MAX471 RS-
  GND                     -> ESP32 GND (Common)
  IN1  (Logic)            -> T-Display-S3 GPIO 13 (PWM Channel 0)
  IN2  (Logic)            -> T-Display-S3 GPIO 12 (PWM Channel 1)
  OUT1 (Power)            -> JGA12-N20 Red Wire (Motor Positive +)
  OUT2 (Power)            -> JGA12-N20 White Wire (Motor Negative -)
  SLP (Sleep)             -> ESP32 3.3V (Must be HIGH to enable driver)

TECHNICAL NOTES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMMON GROUND: The Battery (-), ESP32 GND, MAX471 GND, and DRV8833 GND must
   all be connected together for the signal references to work correctly.

2. VOLTAGE DIVIDERS: The MAX471 has an internal voltage divider that divides 
   the input voltage by 5. At a maximum battery voltage of 4.2V (fully charged 
   18650), VT will output ~0.84V, which is well within the ESP32's 3.3V ADC limit.

3. CURRENT LIMITS: The MAX471 outputs 1V per 1A. Ensure your motor does not 
   exceed 3A peak, or you may exceed the ESP32's 3.3V ADC limit on the AT pin.

4. SIGNAL INTEGRITY: For the PWM lines (13 and 12), keep wires short to 
   minimize electromagnetic interference (EMI) that could affect the 
   analog readings on pins 1 and 2.

5. DECOUPLING: If the display flickers or the ESP32 reboots when the motor
   starts, add a 100uF to 470uF electrolytic capacitor across the
   DRV8833 VCC and GND pins to handle current spikes.

6. DUAL-VOLTAGE MONITORING & USB CHARGING DETECTION:
   - Onboard Battery ADC (GPIO 4): The T-Display-S3 board has a built-in
     100k/100k voltage divider connected to GPIO 4 to read battery level.
   - Onboard Power Control (GPIO 15): Gated power to the LCD screen and internal 
     circuits. GPIO 15 must be driven HIGH at all times during operation.
   - Dual-Sensor Comparison:
     * When USB is disconnected (battery power): Both the internal ADC (GPIO 4)
       and external MAX471 (GPIO 1) read identical voltage levels (within 0.1V).
     * When USB is connected: The onboard charging circuit applies power
       to the board's VBAT line, causing the internal ADC (GPIO 4) to read
       significantly higher (typically > 4.25V, or > 0.12V above the actual 
       battery pack voltage read by the external MAX471).
     * Charging Status: The system dynamically compares these two readings
       to determine if the USB is plugged in and the battery is charging,
       triggering the "CHG" logo on the TFT display and detailed status on the web.
