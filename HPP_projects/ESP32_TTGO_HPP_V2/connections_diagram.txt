--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WIRING TABLE REFERENCE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INA219 SENSOR:
  VIN+ (Positive Input)   -> 18650 Battery (+)
  VIN- (Positive Output)  -> DRV8833 VCC
  SDA                     -> ESP32 GPIO 21 (I2C SDA)
  SCL                     -> ESP32 GPIO 22 (I2C SCL)
  VCC                     -> ESP32 3.3V
  GND                     -> ESP32 GND (Common)

DRV8833 MOTOR DRIVER:
  VCC                     -> INA219 VIN-
  GND                     -> ESP32 GND (Common)
  IN1  (Logic)            -> ESP32 GPIO 13 (PWM Channel 0)
  IN2  (Logic)            -> ESP32 GPIO 12 (PWM Channel 1)
  OUT1 (Power)            -> JGA12-N20 Red Wire (Motor Positive +)
  OUT2 (Power)            -> JGA12-N20 White Wire (Motor Negative -)
  SLP (Sleep)             -> ESP32 3.3V (Must be HIGH to enable driver)

JGA12-N20 ENCODER MOTOR (WIRE MAPPING):
  Red Wire    (Motor +)   -> DRV8833 OUT1
  White Wire  (Motor -)   -> DRV8833 OUT2
  Black Wire  (Enc VCC)   -> ESP32 3.3V (Encoder Power)
  Blue Wire   (Enc GND)   -> ESP32 GND (Common Ground)
  Yellow Wire (Channel A) -> ESP32 GPIO 25 (High-speed interrupt pin)
  Green Wire  (Channel B) -> ESP32 GPIO 26 (State/Direction pin)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECHNICAL NOTES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMMON GROUND: The Battery (-), ESP32 GND, INA219 GND, and DRV8833 GND must
   all be connected together for the signal references to work correctly.

2. I2C COMMUNICATION: The INA219 communicates digitally over I2C. Ensure SDA is
   connected to GPIO 21 and SCL to GPIO 22. Pull-up resistors (e.g., 4.7kΩ) on 
   the SDA/SCL lines may be required if they are not included on your INA219 breakout board.

3. CURRENT & VOLTAGE LIMITS: The INA219 default configuration supports up to 26V
   bus voltage and ±3.2A continuous current. It handles the 18650 voltage (4.2V max) 
   and the motor stall current safely without analog scaling issues.

4. SIGNAL INTEGRITY: Keep the I2C wires (SDA/SCL) relatively short and away from
   the DRV8833 motor output wires to prevent motor PWM noise from corrupting 
   the digital I2C sensor communications.

5. DECOUPLING: If the display flickers or the ESP32 reboots when the motor
   starts, add a 100uF to 470uF electrolytic capacitor across the
   DRV8833 VCC and GND pins to handle current spikes.

6. DUAL-VOLTAGE MONITORING & USB CHARGING DETECTION:
   - Onboard Battery ADC (GPIO 34): The TTGO T-Display board has a built-in
     100k/100k voltage divider connected to GPIO 34 to read battery level.
   - Onboard ADC Enable (GPIO 14): Power to the voltage divider is controlled
     by GPIO 14. GPIO 14 must be driven HIGH to perform a reading, and LOW 
     to save power.
   - Dual-Sensor Comparison:
     * When USB is disconnected (battery power): Both the internal ADC (GPIO 34)
       and external INA219 read identical voltage levels (within 0.1V).
     * When USB is connected: The onboard TP4054 lithium charger applies power
       to the board's VBAT line, causing the internal ADC (GPIO 34) to read
       significantly higher (typically > 4.25V, or > 0.12V above the actual 
       battery pack voltage read by the external INA219 over I2C).
     * Charging Status: The system dynamically compares these two readings
       to determine if the USB is plugged in and the battery is charging,
       triggering the "CHG" logo on the TFT display and detailed status on the web.
