SECTION 1: SURGICAL PEN SENDER PINOUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This unit handles the MJPEG/ESP-NOW stream and the surgical pen hardware. 
Choose the pinout based on your `SELECTED_BOARD` in `BoardConfig.h`.

--- OPTION A: ESP32-WROVER-E (Standard Dev Board) ---
* [ Camera Module (OV2640) ]
* D0 - D7   : GPIO 4, 5, 18, 19, 36, 39, 34, 35
* XCLK      : GPIO 21
* PCLK      : GPIO 22
* VSYNC     : GPIO 25
* HREF      : GPIO 23
* SDA (SCCB): GPIO 26
* SCL (SCCB): GPIO 27
* PWDN      : GPIO 32
* RESET     : -1 (Not Connected)
* FLASH LED : GPIO 4 (Shared with D0 on standard modules)
* [ Motor Driver (DRV8833) ]
* IN1 (Forward) : GPIO 2
* IN2 (Reverse) : GPIO 13
* [ Telemetry I2C ]
* SDA           : GPIO 14
* SCL           : GPIO 15

--- OPTION B: ESP32-CAM (AI-Thinker Module) ---
* [ Camera Module (OV2640) ]
* D0 - D7   : GPIO 5, 18, 19, 21, 36, 39, 34, 35
* XCLK      : GPIO 0
* PCLK      : GPIO 22
* VSYNC     : GPIO 25
* HREF      : GPIO 23
* SDA (SCCB): GPIO 26
* SCL (SCCB): GPIO 27
* PWDN      : GPIO 32
* RESET     : -1
* FLASH LED : GPIO 4 (PWM Brightness Supported)
* [ Motor Driver (DRV8833) ]
* IN1 (Forward) : GPIO 2
* IN2 (Reverse) : GPIO 12
* [ Telemetry I2C ]
* SDA           : GPIO 14
* SCL           : GPIO 15

--- SENSOR COMMON CONNECTIONS ---
* VCC / VM      : Battery/Power Rail (Max 10V)
* GND           : Common Ground
* [ MAX471 -> ADS1115 ]
* AT (Current)  : GPIO 32 (ADC1 - High-precision sampling)
* VT (Voltage)  : GPIO 34 (ADC1 - Battery monitoring)
* RS+ / RS-     : In-line with Motor Power Supply

SECTION 2: HMI RECEIVER (CROWPANEL S3 2.8")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This unit handles the HUD, Menu, and User Interaction.

[ Display (ST7789 Parallel/SPI) ]
* BCLK (SCLK)   : GPIO 42
* MOSI          : GPIO 39
* DC            : GPIO 41
* CS            : GPIO 40
* Backlight     : GPIO 38

[ Touch Controller (GT911 / FT5x06) ]
* SDA           : GPIO 15
* SCL           : GPIO 16
* INT           : GPIO 47
* RST           : GPIO 1 (Output) / 2 (High Trigger)

[ Audio Output ]
* Buzzer        : GPIO 8 (PWM)
* Speaker BCLK  : GPIO 13
* Speaker LRC   : GPIO 11
* Speaker DOUT  : GPIO 12
* Speaker Mute  : GPIO 21 (Active LOW to enable sound)

[ Audio Input ]
* Mic BCLK      : GPIO 9
* Mic WS        : GPIO 3
* Mic DIN       : GPIO 10

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 3: COMMUNICATION SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WiFi Mode     : AP_STA (Sender) / STA (Receiver)
* Protocol      : ESP-NOW (Uncompressed Chunks) & MJPEG over HTTP (Port 80)
* Channel       : 1 (Fixed for low-latency synchronization)
* Frame Request : 0x02 (Pull Strategy)
