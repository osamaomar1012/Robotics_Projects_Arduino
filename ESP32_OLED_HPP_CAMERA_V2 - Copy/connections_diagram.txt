--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WIRING TABLE REFERENCE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAX471 SENSOR:            | WROVER PIN | NOTES
--------------------------|------------|----------------------------------------
  RS+ (Positive Input)    | BATT (+)   | From 18650 Battery Positive
  RS- (Positive Output)   | DRV VCC    | To DRV8833 VCC
  VT  (Voltage Signal)    | GPIO 33    | Analog Input (ADC1: WiFi/Camera Immune)
  AT  (Current Signal)    | GPIO 39    | Analog Input (ADC1: WiFi/Camera Immune)
  GND                     | GND        | Common System Ground

DRV8833 MOTOR DRIVER:     | WROVER PIN | NOTES
--------------------------|------------|----------------------------------------
  VCC                     | MAX RS-    | Clean power from Sensor RS-
  GND                     | GND        | Common Ground
  IN1 (PWM Speed)         | GPIO 2     | LEDC Channel 0
  IN2 (Direction)         | GPIO 13    | LEDC Channel 1
  OUT1 / OUT2             | MOTOR      | Terminals for Surgical DC Motor
  EEP / SLP               | 3.3V       | Tied HIGH for continuous bridge enable

OLED DISPLAY (SSD1306):   | WROVER PIN | NOTES
--------------------------|------------|----------------------------------------
  SDA (Data)              | GPIO 14    | Dedicated I2C SDA
  SCL (Clock)             | GPIO 15    | Dedicated I2C SCL
  VCC / GND               | 3.3V / GND | OLED power input

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POWER PIN CONFIGURATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WROVER-E DEV BOARD requires stable 5V (on the 5V pin) or direct 3.3V power.
The OV2640 camera sensor consumes ~70mA; triple-buffering in PSRAM requires
stable voltage rails to prevent jitter in the graft counter.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EMERGENCY FLASHING MODE (If Flash Chip Communication Fails):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Jumper GPIO 0  to GND (Enters Download Mode)
2. Jumper GPIO 12 to GND (Forces 3.3V Flash Voltage - CRITICAL for WROVER)
3. Press EN (RESET) Button
4. Set Arduino IDE Upload Speed to 115200
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TECHNICAL NOTES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMMON GROUND: The Battery (-), ESP32 GND, MAX471 GND, and DRV8833 GND must
   all be connected together for the signal references to work correctly.

2. VOLTAGE LIMITS: The MAX471 outputs a max of 3V when the source voltage is
   15V (1/5 ratio). At 4.2V (fully charged 18650), VT will output ~0.84V, 
   which is well within the ESP32's 3.3V ADC limit.
   
3. ADC1 RELIABILITY: Pins 32-39 are ADC1. We use 33 and 39 for sensors. 
   This ensures 100% telemetry accuracy during high-speed video streaming.

4. CAMERA PWDN: GPIO 32 is used to control camera power. The software pulls
   this LOW during setup to enable the sensor.

6. BOOT STRAPPING: GPIO 12 (MTDI) must be LOW during boot for 3.3V Flash.
   If flashing fails, tie GPIO 12 to GND.

7. CURRENT LIMITS: The MAX471 outputs 1V per 1A. Ensure your motor does not 
   exceed 3A peak, or you may exceed the ESP32's 3.3V ADC limit on the AT pin.

8. SIGNAL INTEGRITY: For the PWM lines, keep wires short to 
   minimize electromagnetic interference (EMI) that could affect the 
   analog readings on pins 33 and 35.

10. PRO-GRADE UPGRADE: For mission-critical surgical reliability, use an 
    external I2C ADC (e.g., ADS1115) connected to Pins 2 and 4.
